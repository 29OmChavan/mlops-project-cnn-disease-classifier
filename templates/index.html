Project 02: Deep Learning Computer Vision & Clinical Pathology Triage System

What This Project Is & How I Built It: A production Computer Vision & Clinical Decision Support system engineered around a 4-Stage Reproducible DVC Pipeline, ResNet-18 Transfer Learning, Dynamic INT8 Quantization, Spatial Grad-CAM Localization, Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) Epistemic Uncertainty, and a First-Principles 2D Convolution & Micrograd Autograd Engine.

1. First-Principles Autograd & CNN NumPy + Micrograd

Implemented a reverse-mode automatic differentiation engine (ScalarValue) and pure NumPy 2D Cross-Correlation (conv2d_single_channel_from_scratch) + 2D Max-Pooling before scaling to PyTorch ResNet-18.

2. Grad-CAM & TTA Uncertainty Explainable Medical CV

Generates side-by-side spatial saliency / Grad-CAM heatmaps pinpointing focal pathology hotspots alongside $T=6$ Test-Time Augmentation passes to measure epistemic standard deviation and Shannon entropy.

3. DVC DAG & INT8 Quantization DagsHub + MLflow

Tracked end-to-end via dvc.yaml & dvc.lock on DagsHub (`Val Accuracy = 1.00`, `Macro F1 = 1.00`, `ROC-AUC = 1.00`), with Dynamic INT8 Quantization for low-latency CPU container deployment.

5FP32 vs. Dynamic INT8 QuantizationBenchmarks full-precision vs. quantized linear layers for CPU container serving.
61-Click Sample Scan LibraryInstant browser testing across Coccidiosis, Healthy, Newcastle_Disease, and Salmonella.
7Multi-Flock Batch Triage QueueEvaluates cohorts in parallel and sorts patients/flocks by clinical urgency rank.

9Interactive Micrograd Autograd LabComputes forward activations and exact reverse-mode gradients $\partial L/\partial w_i$ live.
10OOD Helmholtz Free Energy DetectorComputes $E(x) = -\log \sum_c \exp(f_c(x))$ to verify in-distribution microscopy inputs.
11AI Pathologist Second-Opinion AgentSynthesizes confidence, uncertainty, hotspot coverage, pathogen etiology, and lab orders.
