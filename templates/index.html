Part 1: Reproducible 4-Stage DVC Setup

This repository splits poultry microscopy classification into 4 DVC stages (`data_ingestion`, `prepare_base_model`, `training`, `evaluation`) so `dvc repro` runs cleanly from a fresh clone.

Part 2: 6-Pass TTA & Input-Gradient Maps

Each slide is evaluated across 6 augmented views (flips, contrast/brightness shifts, and blur) to measure prediction variance before issuing a clinical triage call.
